## OVERVIEW

Moore Awareness is a mission-driven platform at the intersection of advocacy, creative storytelling, spiritual growth, and futurism. The ecosystem operates as an integrated whole: each piece reinforces the others rather than competing.

---

## THE ECOSYSTEM

| Entity | Role |
|--------|------|
| Moore Awareness LLC | Umbrella brand tying everything together |
| MSAP (Moore's Self-Awareness Program) | Nonprofit arm — holistic recovery methodology |
| So Brrr Water | Social enterprise product line — funds the mission |
| So Brrr Streak App | Daily tools for personal growth |
| Nexa | Steven's personal AI assistant — operates as his internal system for brand development, execution, and thinking partner (not a public product) |
| Podcast | Thought-provoking, heart-centered storytelling |
| Comics & D&D Integration | Storytelling tools that bridge culture, healing, imagination |

**Design principle:** if each piece reinforces the others, it becomes a self-sustaining system.
