## STRUCTURE FRAMEWORK

### Status
- **Now:** self-funded accumulation vehicle, internal only
- **Later:** optional outside capital only after proven deployment and governance

### Legal / Entity
- **Current form:** Minnesota single-member LLC
- **Outside capital gate:** add operating agreement provisions and subscription materials before bringing in any investor
- **Conversion path:** single-member LLC → multi-member LLC or LP at first outside close
- **Governance:** Steven sole member/manager until external capital is possible

### Capital Flow Model

**Why this matters:** Moore Capital is not a side project. It is the financial engine that funds everything else in the ecosystem. Every downstream layer — social impact, AI infrastructure, operating businesses — depends on capital flow from this layer.

**How it works:**
- So Brrr Water, merch, and app revenue feed Moore Capital
- Moore Capital allocates capital back into:
  1. Moore Awareness LLC — social impact and program development
  2. Nexa — AI infrastructure and decision support
  3. Future ventures — So Brrr Water, recovery platform, media, etc.
- Remainder can deploy externally if aligned with Moore pillars

**Rules:**
- Minimum percentage of net profit routed to Moore Capital reserve
- Reserve funds are not borrowed against for lifestyle
- Negative revenue periods do not reverse prior deposits
- Moore Capital does not cannibalize itself to fund operations without a repayment plan

### Investment Thesis

**Ecosystem-first deployment:**
1. Internal: Moore Awareness LLC programs and infrastructure
2. Internal: operating businesses (So Brrr Water, app, media)
3. External: Black-founded ventures in tech, wellness, culture, and recovery
4. Legacy: real estate, land, generational vehicles

**Constraints:**
- Max single position size relative to total fund
- No speculative leverage without documented risk tolerance
- Every external investment must map to at least one Moore pillar

### First Deployment Candidate
- **Internal priority:** MSAP framework completion and first cohort
- **Operating priority:** So Brrr Water scale if unit economics allow
- **External:** one angel check per quarter maximum during accumulation phase

## COMPLIANCE

### Securities Regulation
- **Now:** solo-member vehicle is not making a public securities offering
- **First outside capillary triggers:**
  - investor suitability documentation
  - subscription agreement
  - Minnesota Blue Sky / federal notice filings if applicable
  - outside capital only after Minn. securities counsel review

### Tax & Entity
- Minnesota single-member LLC default pass-through on personal return
- Add separate EIN and bank account immediately upon formation
- Consider checking the box to taxed as S-Corp when revenue justifies payroll
- Do not commingle personal and fund capital
- Maintain separate bank account, bookkeeping, and records from Moore Awareness

### Operating Rules
- Operating agreement governs contributions, distributions, voting, and dissolution
- Written investment memo for every deploy above threshold
- Quarterly internal review, not necessarily external audit until fund crosses meaningful size
